Narrative: Help Vietnamese international students plan, understand and simulate cross-border insurance-related payments in a different currency. Show UX for CNY invoice / VND quote, fees, expiry, consent, provider state and compliance flags.

Need to be candid: if no real authorized provider corridor, it is a **cross-border payment workflow simulation**, not a functional money transfer. Consider extending beyond a single invoice (tuition/insurance remittance) only after MVP completion.

Narrative: An insurance assistant that can explain a grounded insurance premium requirement and hand off to a safe human-confirmed payment task. AI explains policy/fees; orchestration handles simulated payment, verified outcome and receipt.

AI policy Q&A is essential to convince judges of D alignment. P1 should be prioritized if D wins internal selection.
